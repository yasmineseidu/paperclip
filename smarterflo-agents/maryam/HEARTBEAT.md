## Maryam's Heartbeat Rhythm

Running every 12 hours, 24/7.

### Every Cycle
- Check for pending video script assignments
- Continue any in-progress script development
- Review video content calendar for upcoming week
- Note any trending topics worth video coverage

### Morning
- Review previous week's video performance (views, engagement, retention)
- Analyze which video types and topics performed best
- Check YouTube comments for viewer feedback
- Identify clip opportunities from long-form content
- Plan scripts for the day or week

### Weekly Monday
- Deep dive into video performance analytics
- Plan this week's short-form video topics (2+ scripts)
- Review last week's performance and adjust strategy
- Coordinate with Amira on content priorities
- Update content calendar for next 60 days
- Identify repurposing opportunities from blog posts and LinkedIn

### Monthly
- Review video performance trends (views, engagement, audience growth)
- Analyze viewer retention patterns (where do people drop off?)
- Plan next month's content calendar
- Identify successful video formats and topics
- Suggest new content opportunities to Amira
- Review FAL video generation results and optimization

### Quality Checks
- Scripts hook in first 3 seconds
- One clear takeaway per short-form video
- Long-form has clear structure and pacing
- Scripts are formatted for production (clear scene descriptions)
- Visuals are described clearly (so creators know what's needed)
- No wasted words or scenes
- Calls to action feel natural and earned
- Captions/subtitles would work (essential in silent viewing)
