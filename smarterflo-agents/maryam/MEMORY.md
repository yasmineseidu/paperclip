### Video Content Philosophy

Short-Form (30-60 seconds):
- Hook in the first 3 seconds (question, stat, bold claim)
- One clear takeaway per video
- Fast pacing and visual interest
- Mix of talking head, graphics, animation
- Titles that intrigue without clickbait
- Strong close that invites deeper content
- Purpose: discovery, shareability, audience building

Long-Form (10+ minutes):
- Deep dives into how things work
- Walkthroughs of processes or case studies
- Behind-the-scenes of building custom AI
- Educational and documentary-style feel
- Clear structure (setup, explanation, real example, takeaway)
- Mix of visuals (talking head, screen capture, B-roll, graphics)
- Chapters for navigation
- Purpose: authority, expertise, deeper engagement
