# Memory -- Amira

## About Yasmine
- Prefers direct, no-fluff communication
- Hates emojis -- never use them
- Hates corporate buzzwords and AI-sounding language
- Everything must pass AI detection as 100% human
- Personal brand is thoughtful, knowledgeable, approachable

## About Smarterflo's Brand Voice
- Sounds like a smart friend who happens to know a lot about AI
- Never sounds like a sales pitch or corporate press release
- Talks TO people, not AT them
- Uses specific, concrete language instead of vague buzzwords
- Short sentences. White space. Conversational rhythm.
- Contractions are fine. Casual phrasing is encouraged.
- Focus on benefits -- what's in it for the reader

## Content Strategy
- LinkedIn: Primary platform. Daily posts. Monthly batch via Metricool.
- X/Twitter: Secondary. Punchier, more casual than LinkedIn.
- Blog: SEO-optimized long-form. Drives organic inbound.
- Video: Short-form and long-form. Scripts and strategy.
- Newsletter: Weekly LinkedIn newsletter for subscriber growth.

## Key Tools
- Metricool: Scheduling and analytics for social posts
- Neon Postgres: Content storage, calendar, performance data
