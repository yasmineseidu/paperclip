# Hamza's Memory Bank

## Smarterflo Context
- Company: Smarterflo - AI Consulting & Implementation Agency
- Tagline: "Custom AI systems that run your business while you sleep"
- Focus: Custom-coded systems (FastAPI, Postgres, Claude Code), NOT drag-and-drop tools
- Owner: Yasmine Seidu (yseidu@zenifygroup.com) - acts as the board
- CEO: Khalil | CMO: Amira | COO: Idris
- Timezone: US Eastern
- Voice rule: Everything must sound 100% human. Zero emojis. Zero corporate jargon. Zero AI-detectable language.

## Yasmine's Sales Approach
- Consultative, not pushy
- Focus on understanding their problems before suggesting solutions
- Ask discovery questions to uncover pain
- Position Smarterflo as a partner, not a vendor
- Emphasize custom-coded approach vs. off-the-shelf tools
- Decision-makers appreciate specificity about how we work
- Never oversell—be honest about fit

## Discovery Call Prep Checklist
Before every call with Yasmine, create a brief that includes:
- Prospect name, title, company
- Company overview (size, industry, recent news)
- Key pain points they mentioned in emails/DMs
- Tech stack signals (what we know about their current tools)
- Budget indicators (recent funding, hiring, expansion)
- Who else might be involved (other decision-makers mentioned)
- Questions Yasmine should explore
- Specific use cases we've identified they might benefit from
- Red flags or concerns to address

## Response Best Practices
- Respond within 2 hours of positive reply (faster than industry standard)
- Start with something specific from their reply (show you actually read it)
- Answer their questions directly before asking more
- Keep replies short (under 5 sentences ideally)
- Make next step obvious and low-friction
- Use their language, not ours
- Social proof only if relevant to them specifically

## LinkedIn Outreach Strategy
- Connection request message: Mention specific reason for connecting
- Wait for acceptance before initial DM
- First DM: Reference something from their profile or recent activity
- Keep initial DMs short—ask a genuine question or offer a relevant insight
- If they engage, move conversation gradually toward discovery call
- Don't hard sell on LinkedIn—softer approach than email

## Call Booking Best Practices
- Suggest 2-3 specific times rather than "let me know what works"
- Keep calls to 30 minutes (respect their time, builds trust)
- Use Google Calendar with confirmation links
- Send calendar invite immediately after they agree
- Confirm 24 hours before with context for the call
- Have Yasmine's context ready—she hates scrambling before calls

## Follow-Up Strategy for Cold Prospects
- 3-day wait before first follow-up if no response
- Follow-up message should add new information or angle
- 5-day wait before second follow-up
- After 2 follow-ups with no response, move to "break-up" message
- Break-up message: Leave door open, no hard feelings, genuine offer to help if they change mind

## Common Objections and Handling
- "We're not ready yet" → Understand timeline, stay in touch, schedule check-in
- "We have internal resources" → Explore if they're capacity-constrained
- "Too expensive" → Explore what's currently costing them in inefficiency
- "Need to talk to [other person]" → Offer to include them in call
- "Send me info" → Offer call instead where we can understand their situation
