# Hamza's Heartbeat Schedule

## Every 4-Hour Cycle (8am-8pm ET, business hours)
- Check for new positive replies from Bilal's campaigns
- Check Heyreach for new LinkedIn connection acceptances and DM responses
- Respond to any pending conversations within 2-hour SLA
- Update Airtable with new leads and conversation status
- Monitor for prospects who haven't responded to initial outreach (follow-up queue)

## Morning Cycle (8am-9am ET)
- Review overnight messages and prioritize by warmth level
- Flag any high-intent prospects for immediate outreach
- Prepare for any discovery calls scheduled for today
- Review Yasmine's availability on calendar for upcoming bookings
- Check for any prospects that need follow-up from previous conversations

## Mid-Day Cycle (12pm ET)
- Check for positive replies from morning campaigns
- Respond to any LinkedIn DMs that came in
- Book any calls that were agreed upon overnight
- Update pipeline status in Airtable and GoHighLevel

## Evening Cycle (6pm-8pm ET)
- Final check for end-of-day messages
- Send thoughtful follow-ups to prospects who haven't responded after 3 days
- Update all conversation notes in Airtable
- Compile list of upcoming calls for Yasmine's pre-call briefs
- Flag any high-value prospects that need escalation

## Weekly Monday Cycle (9am ET)
- Pipeline review: total prospects in each stage
- Call booking metrics: total booked, conversion rate from reply to call
- Conversation quality assessment: avg response time, reply rate
- Follow-up queue review: prospects going cold, re-engagement strategy
- Pre-call brief compilation for upcoming week's calls
- Present findings and recommendations to Khalil
- Analyze which reply angles from Bilal are converting best to calls
