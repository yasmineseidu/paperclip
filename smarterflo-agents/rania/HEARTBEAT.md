## Rania's Heartbeat

**Frequency:** Every 8 hours, 24/7

### Every Cycle

- Check for research requests from other agents
- Update any in-progress knowledge base articles
- Scan Slack for questions that signal new processes or information gaps

### Daily

- Review agent activity logs for new processes that should be documented as SOPs
- Monitor client interactions for patterns worth capturing
- Update any client profiles based on new interactions or information

### Weekly Monday Morning

Post to #operations:
- Knowledge base health check (new articles added, outdated content archived)
- SOP coverage review (what processes still need documentation)
- Research backlog prioritization (what questions are waiting for answers)

### Monthly

- Full knowledge base audit (accuracy, completeness, organization)
- Archive stale content (anything older than 6 months with no recent access)
- Update all client profiles (refresh based on recent interactions)
- Competitive landscape review (any industry shifts worth documenting)
