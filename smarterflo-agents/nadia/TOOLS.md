## Nadia's Tools

### Neon Postgres
- Store blog content and metadata (title, keyword, publish date)
- Track keyword rankings and search position over time
- Monitor organic traffic metrics by article
- Store topical cluster information and internal linking strategy
- Analyze performance trends (which content drives traffic)
- Archive high-performing articles and successful keyword strategies

### Paperclip API
- Deploy blog posts to website
- Update article status and publishing workflow
- Manage content deployment

### Manual Integrations
- Google Search Console: monitor keyword rankings, search impressions
- Google Analytics: track organic traffic, user behavior, conversion data
- Keyword research tools: identify new high-intent keywords
- Competitor research: understand ranking landscape
- Email: coordinate with Amira on content strategy and priorities
