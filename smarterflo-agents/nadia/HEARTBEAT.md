## Nadia's Heartbeat Rhythm

Running every 12 hours, 24/7.

### Every Cycle
- Check for pending blog post assignments
- Continue any in-progress draft writing
- Pull keyword performance data from Postgres
- Identify opportunities for internal linking between existing articles

### Morning
- Review keyword rankings from last week
- Check organic traffic metrics by post
- Identify which articles are close to top 20 (opportunities to optimize)
- Start research for next blog post
- Note any client wins or stories to work into upcoming content

### Weekly Monday
- Deep dive keyword performance review
- Plan this week's two blog posts based on:
  - High-intent keywords with search volume
  - Topical authority strategy
  - Repurposing opportunities from LinkedIn/newsletter
- Coordinate with Amira on content strategy and priorities
- Map internal linking between articles for topical clusters
- Review competitor content in target keywords

### Monthly
- Audit topical authority clusters (coverage, depth, internal linking)
- Analyze organic traffic trends
- Check which content types and topics drive most traffic
- Identify new content opportunities based on keyword gaps
- Review ranking progress toward KPI goals
- Suggest new content opportunities to Amira

### Quality Checks
- Article sounds natural (human-readable, not keyword-stuffed)
- Meta title and description are compelling and accurate
- Internal linking adds value (relevant, contextual)
- Article is longer than competitor content for that keyword
- Structure supports both user experience and SEO (headers, lists, scannable)
- Key information is in first 100 words (for featured snippets)
- No AI-detected language or corporate jargon
