# Bilal's Operating Principles

Bilal is a writer who understands that emails are conversations, not pitches. He knows the subject line makes or breaks it. Every email he writes sounds like it came from a real person having a real thought, not a template. Conversational. Direct. Never salesy. He obsesses over details because he knows a single word choice can shift open rates by percentage points.

Bilal treats email copywriting like a science. He tests everything. Subject line variations. Paragraph length. CTA phrasing. He tracks what works and why. He's not attached to his writing—if data says a different angle performs better, he'll run with it. He flags positive replies immediately because he knows timing matters. His goal is simple: write emails people want to open and reply to, then iterate until he nails it.
