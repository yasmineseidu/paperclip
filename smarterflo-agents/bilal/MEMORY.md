## Yasmine's Email Philosophy
- Conversational and benefit-focused, not salesy
- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max per paragraph)
- Clear CTAs that feel natural, not forced
- Personal touches (reference company news, recent hire, product launch)
- Assume the reader is busy—get to the value fast
- Questions work better than statements
- "We could..." vs "You should..."

## Cold Email Best Practices
- Subject lines should be 3-7 words, no clickbait
- First line should reference something specific about them
- Lead with curiosity or insight, not pitch
- Social proof only if relevant to them specifically
- CTA is one clear action: schedule a call, send a message, reply
- Follow-ups should add new information, not repeat
- Breakup email should be honest and leave door open
- Avoid: exclamation marks, ALL CAPS, buzzwords, generic value props

## Email Sequence Structure
- Email 1: Introduction + specific insight
- Email 2 (3-5 days later): Different angle, proof point
- Email 3 (3-5 days later): Social proof or case study angle
- Email 4 (3-5 days later): Lower-commitment ask (reply with thoughts)
- Email 5 (7 days later): Breakup email

## Campaign Performance Targets
- Open rate: 40%+ is strong (industry avg 20-30%)
- Reply rate: 5%+ is excellent (industry avg 1-3%)
- Bounce rate: Under 3% is acceptable
- Domain warmup: Gradual increase in send volume, monitor reputation

## Known Pitfalls to Avoid
- Sending from cold domains (warm up first)
- Generic subject lines that blend into inbox
- Long first paragraphs (people scroll)
- Asking for too much too soon
- Not personalizing—"Hi there" is a red flag
- Ignoring bounce rates—fix before it kills domain
- Testing too many variables at once

## A/B Test Framework
- Only test ONE variable at a time
- Control: original email
- Test: one change (subject, copy angle, CTA)
- Sample size: At least 100 sends per variation
- Duration: Full sequence completion before declaring winner
- Track: Opens, replies, bounces separately
