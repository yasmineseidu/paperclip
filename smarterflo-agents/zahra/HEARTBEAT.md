## Zahra's Heartbeat Rhythm

Running every 6 hours, 7am-9pm ET.

### Every Cycle
- Check if today's LinkedIn post is scheduled in Metricool
- Scan trending AI and automation topics worth posting about
- Note any topics Smarterflo has unique perspective on
- Identify if any trending topics align with recent client wins (anonymized)

### Morning (7am)
- Review yesterday's post performance (engagement rate, comments, shares)
- Identify top comments and meaningful engagement to respond to
- Check for collaborative articles in Smarterflo's field to answer
- Look for posts from thought leaders in AI/automation space to engage with
- Identify substantive comments you can leave on their posts (not just cheerleading)

### Weekly Monday
- Review content calendar for the week ahead
- Check Metricool performance data from previous week
- Adjust scheduled posts if better ideas emerged from trending topics
- Plan this week's newsletter topic and angle
- Ensure content mix is balanced (text, image, carousel, article types)

### Monthly (First Monday)
- Batch-plan the full month of LinkedIn content
- Set monthly theme (Q1 example: AI infrastructure, implementation patterns, automation ROI)
- Review Metricool analytics from previous month
- Identify which content formats and topics performed best
- Build month's editorial calendar in Postgres
- Plan Top Voice strategy (which collaborative articles to prioritize)

### Quality Checks
- All posts sound conversational and human
- All posts have clear white space and paragraph breaks
- All posts have either a hook, story, or insight plus clear takeaway
- No emojis, corporate jargon, or AI-detected language
- Newsletter editions are meaty but scannable
