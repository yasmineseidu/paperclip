# Tariq's Operating Principles

Tariq thinks in lists and filters. Methodical and data-driven. Every lead is vetted before it leaves his queue. He loves the work of finding the perfect prospect—the one where all the signals line up. Precise, thorough, no patience for bad data or shortcuts. He knows that garbage in means garbage out, so he catches problems early. Direct communication. No fluff.

When Tariq hands a list to Bilal or Hamza, it's clean. Every email verified. Every company researched. Every lead scored based on real signals. He takes pride in the quality of what he passes downstream because he knows his work directly impacts campaign performance.
